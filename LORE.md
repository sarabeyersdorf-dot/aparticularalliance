## The name

An alliance is a few parties who are better together than apart. In every home I touch, the alliance is three: the client, the maker, and me, the one who brings them together. The makers bring the craft. I bring the eye. You get a home that finally reflects your own particular taste.

**Members of the Alliance:** our makers are not vendors. They are members, chosen for their craft and named on every piece they make.

**The one-line version:** *A Particular Alliance creates homes for people who won't settle for builder grade.*

## Where it began

I have walked through hundreds of homes as a real estate broker. Some of them sold for well over a million dollars. And again and again, I noticed the same thing.

The square footage was there. The view was there. The price was there. But the details told a different story. The same plastic switch plates. The same stamped vent covers. The same light fixtures you could find in any new subdivision. Beautiful homes, wearing builder-grade clothes.

Nobody did anything wrong. Builders choose what is fast and affordable, and most buyers never get the chance to choose otherwise. They move in, they get busy, and the house never quite becomes theirs.

At the same time, I kept meeting extraordinary makers. Metalworkers, ceramicists, woodworkers, glass artists, people making one-of-a-kind pieces by hand, often just a few miles from the homes that needed them.

Great homes and great makers have always needed each other: homes waiting for character, and makers waiting for the right home for their work. What was missing was someone with the eye to bring them together, on purpose. That's the particular in the Alliance.

## What we believe

- **The details are the home.** People remember the brass pull warm under their hand, the light that glows instead of glares, the switch plate that feels like jewelry. Square footage is forgotten. Details are not.
- **Nothing should be repeated.** If you can find it in a catalog, it does not belong on our list. Every piece we place is handmade, limited, or made for you alone.
- **Makers deserve a patron.** Behind every piece is a person with a name and a craft. We pay them fairly and tell their stories.
- **Taste can be borrowed, then owned.** You do not need to know where to find a hand-forged bronze vent cover. That is my job. Once it is in your home, it is yours.
- **The credit belongs to you.** We work quietly. Our name is not on the wall. Yours is.
- **Done means done.** We source, price, deliver, and install. You do not chase vendors or contractors. You walk in, and it is finished.

## How it works

Every home we touch is an alliance of three: the client, the maker, and me, the one who brings them together.

1. **The walk-through.** I visit your home and look for the builder-grade details hiding in plain sight. We talk about how you live and how you want the house to feel.
2. **The edit.** I select pieces from our makers, and commission new ones when nothing existing is right. You see them as a curated proposal, one room or one collection at a time.
3. **The price.** You get one clear number: pieces, delivery, and installation. No open-ended designer bill.
4. **The install.** Our licensed installers put every piece in place. You come home to it finished.
5. **The story.** Each piece arrives with a card naming its maker and how it was made, so you can tell its story to your guests.

**The client** gets a home that reads as their own exceptional taste. **The maker** gets a patron and a home for their work. **I** stay in the background, making sure every detail is right. Together, that is the alliance.

## The collections

Each collection is a mood, so clients can choose by feeling instead of by catalog number. Collection names still need a quick conflict check before anything is printed.

### Gilt & Grain

*The touchpoints.* Warm brass meets hand-finished wood in the places your hands go every day: cabinet pulls, door levers, switch plates, house numbers. This is where most homes begin with us, because it changes how a house feels within a single afternoon.

### Ore & Oak

*Gold Country, made by hand.* Pieces from makers in the Sierra foothills, where gold once ran through quartz and oak still covers the hills. Forged iron, reclaimed oak, native stone. Rooted, honest, and a little rugged.

### Brass & Burl

*The statement.* Sculptural lighting, rare figured woods, and the one piece every guest asks about. This is our highest tier, often commissioned from scratch.

### Gilt & Loam

*Earth, refined.* Hand-thrown ceramics, plaster finishes, textured walls, and pottery lamps. Soft color and natural texture for homes that have been beige too long.

### Marble & Moss

*Stone and living things.* Carved stone vessels, bath and kitchen accents, planters, and pieces that bring the outdoors in. Cool, calm, and quietly luxurious.

## Voice and language

We sound like a trusted friend with impeccable taste: warm, confident, unhurried. Never salesy, never stuffy, and never critical of the client's home. The builder chose those details, not them.

| Use | Avoid |
| --- | --- |
| Commissioned, handmade, one of a kind | Bespoke, curated (overused) |
| Maker, artisan, by name | Vendor, supplier, product |
| Your home, your eye | Our design, our look |
| Investment, heirloom | Deal, discount, affordable luxury |
| Builder grade (about the house) | Generic, boring (about the owner) |
